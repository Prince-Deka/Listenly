# Listenly — Video Recorder with Live Captions

Listenly is a browser-based video recording app that captures camera video and microphone audio while generating live speech-to-text captions.

The captions are displayed as an overlay during recording and are embedded directly into the downloaded video. A timestamped text transcript can also be downloaded separately.

## Features

- Record video from your camera with microphone audio
- Generate live speech transcription while recording
- Burn live captions directly into the recorded video
- Download captioned recordings as `.webm`
- Download timestamped transcripts as `.txt`
- Switch between front and rear cameras before recording
- Choose from multiple speech-recognition languages
- No account, backend, database, or hosting required
- Files are created and saved locally in the browser

## Requirements

For the best experience, use a current version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Camera and microphone permissions are required. Live transcription depends on the browser’s Speech Recognition support and may require an internet connection.
